# Figure captures

These PNGs are the original captures extracted, byte for byte, from the
previous `teaser.svg` and `edit.svg`. The figure generator changes their
layout and labels, not their pixels.

`teaser-0.png` through `teaser-5.png` show Ray, Voxel, Sliced, Scattered,
Painted, and Mesh, in that order. `edit-0.png` through `edit-5.png` show
the before/after pairs for Ray, Voxel, and Mesh.
