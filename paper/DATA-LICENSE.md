# Data-rights release gate

The code is Apache-2.0. Original *Vangers* level archives, converted terrain,
screenshots, benchmark depth buffers, and measurements derived from those
levels need a separate documented grant before public artifact release.

## Executed grant

- [ ] Record the rights holder and authorized signatory.
- [ ] Record the execution date, grant title, and stable copy/location.
- [ ] Quote or cite the governing license terms in the paper and artifact.
- [ ] Confirm that publication figures and supplemental video are permitted.
- [ ] Confirm that converted terrain and derived measurements may be shared.
- [ ] If terrain or derived data are supplemental files, confirm that their
      redistribution terms satisfy JCGT's non-restrictive open-data rule;
      otherwise omit them and document the user-supplied-data workflow.
- [ ] Confirm whether original archives may be redistributed or only fetched
      or supplied by the user.
- [ ] Confirm commercial/non-commercial, geographic, and time restrictions.
- [ ] Confirm attribution, trademark, notice, and takedown requirements.

## Content scope

- [ ] Fostral is named explicitly; record the archive version and SHA-256.
- [ ] Common palette/configuration data required to render Fostral is covered.
- [ ] The other nine worlds in the fit survey are named explicitly, or remove
      their rows and claims from the camera-ready paper.
- [ ] Verify that no music, character art, fonts, or unrelated game assets are
      included in the benchmark bundle.

## Release check

- [ ] Add the final data license and attribution files to the artifact.
- [ ] Make the paper's Data availability text match the executed terms.
- [ ] Test the artifact from a clean checkout using only redistributable inputs.
- [ ] If any item remains unresolved, ship only the engine/converter/harness
      and document the lawfully-obtained user-data workflow accepted by the
      venue.
